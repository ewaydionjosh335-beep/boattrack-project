Quick Guest Registration (Walk-in)

Walk-in Registration Guide

Use this portal to instantly register walk-in passengers who do not have an existing BoatTrack account or mobile app.

  • Instant QR Code: Generates a permanent boarding QR code upon registration.
  • Accurate Weight: Enter the passenger's exact weight in kg (1–300 kg) for vessel manifest load calculations.
  • Direct Boarding: After generation, you can directly proceed to assign the guest to an active boarding station or print/download their QR code.
